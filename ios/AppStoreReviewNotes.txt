App Store Review Notes — WOFINS iOS (IAP)

Demo account
- Use the owner/admin account provided in App Review Information (company owner).
- The account must belong to a company so subscription management is available.

How to test In-App Purchase
1. Sign in with the demo owner account.
2. If the company subscription is expired, the paywall is shown immediately.
3. Or open Account → Paket Aktif to see “Kelola paket”.
4. Purchase any of: Starter / Professional / Business (Monthly or Yearly) using a Sandbox Apple ID.
5. After purchase, dashboard access refreshes for the company.
6. Use “Pulihkan pembelian” to restore.

Multiplatform (Guideline 3.1.3(b))
- WOFINS is a multiplatform B2B SaaS (web + iOS).
- Subscriptions purchased on the web continue to unlock the same digital features in iOS.
- The same plan tiers are also available for purchase in iOS via In-App Purchase.

Product IDs (auto-renewable subscriptions — group “WOFINS Plans”)
- wofins.starter.monthly / wofins.starter.yearly
- wofins.professional.monthly / wofins.professional.yearly
- wofins.business.monthly / wofins.business.yearly

Server Notifications V2
- https://app.wofins.id/api/v1/billing/apple/notifications
